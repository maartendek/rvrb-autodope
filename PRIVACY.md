# Privacy Policy for AutoDope

**Last Updated:** 19-10-2026

## Overview
AutoDope is a Chrome extension that automatically votes up tracks on app.rvrb.one. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection
AutoDope collects and stores **minimal data**:

### Local Storage Only
- **AutoDope enabled/disabled state** - Stored locally on your device using Chrome's `storage.local` API
- This data is **never** sent to external servers
- This data is **never** shared with third parties

### No External Communication
- AutoDope does **not** collect any personal information
- AutoDope does **not** send data to any external servers
- AutoDope does **not** track your browsing activity
- AutoDope does **not** use analytics or tracking services
- AutoDope does **not** store cookies

### What AutoDope Can Access
The extension only accesses:
- The DOM of `app.rvrb.one` to find and click the vote button
- Your local Chrome storage to remember your preference

## Permissions Explained

| Permission | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| `storage` | Saves your AutoDope on/off preference locally |
| `host_permissions: app.rvrb.one` | Allows the extension to run on app.rvrb.one |

## Data Deletion
All data is stored locally on your device. To delete all data:
1. Uninstall the extension from `chrome://extensions/`
2. All stored data will be automatically deleted

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Continued use of the extension constitutes acceptance of any changes.
